Frequently asked

Is my data backed up?
Your dogs, pedigrees, and records are stored on your device. Back up your iPhone regularly (iCloud or a computer backup) to protect against data loss.

Can I share available puppies with buyers?
Yes — from a dog's page, use the Share options to generate a link or social post.
